The selected Step 3 direction has two core solutions: owner-confirmed commitment handoff and lightweight explicit resolution. A detected action remains a candidate until an owner confirms; a suggested replacement must accept before ownership activates. Valid explicit outcomes are Done, accepted Reassignment, and Deliberately dropped. Blocked and pending reassignment are unresolved. Keep this within the existing Tasks experience rather than building another project-management system. The 28% baseline and 45% six-month target are case-study premises, not proven product impact.

**Why:** These are the author's selected and prioritized solutions and measurement rules for the Step 4 capstone; future work should remain consistent with them.

**How to apply:** Use these constraints when revising the prototype, PRD, or later capstone deliverables; distinguish synthetic examples and hypotheses from validated outcomes.
